I have been thinking about restarting my blogging for a long time now; it has been even longer since I last blogged here (or at all). My life looks very different now than it did when I was a 2nd year PhD student, and I don't have the need to consistently write for work, other than code and documentation. 

However, I'm at a point in my career where I am considering next steps and one of the tracks I want to look into is moving towards research in AI safety. This might sound a bit out of the blue, and I will probably write a post on my motivations for this at some point. The TL;DR version is that AI is not going anywhere and I see AI safety as one of the most pressing topics we have to dedicate our resources to. 

The reason why that is relevant to this blog/my blogging in general is that I have been doing some study on my personal time. Until a couple of weeks ago I had not properly read a research paper in over five years; even longer for computer science papers. I realised now that a good way to test my understanding of what I'm reading is to try to explain it, so I will attempt to do that through blog posts. 

Some points for my future reading and writing endeavors:

1. While this is in a public blog, its main purpose is _not_ to be seen as an outreach source. This blog will keep running purely for my own educational purposes.

2. I will not use AI to write. This links to the point above: I want to write to test my understanding, and for that I have to truly sit down and write the actual words myself. For completitude I will also mention here that I will not use AI for reading, meaning at the very least my initial notes on a paper will be completely my own. I will not ask for AI summaries until I'm at a point where I feel I can discuss the concepts and would like a sounding board for my thoughts.

3. I will not track my writing (or will I eventually?). I don't even use the A6 Hobonichi I was tracking in on my previous posts, as I have moved to the Hobonichi weeks --  but that is probably the subject for a blog post on its own :)

4. ...but I will track my reading. For now this is in private lists. I'll try to think of an approach to keep track here.
